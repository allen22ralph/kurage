## v0.3.5: UI overhaul, part 1

The app now has themes, and the first new one, **Depth**, rebuilds Home from top to bottom. This is part 1 of the overhaul: Home is done, and other pages come later.

Nothing changes until you choose it: **Classic**, the look you know, stays the default. To try the new look, go to **Settings > Appearance > Theme** and pick **Depth**.

### Depth's Home
- **The top bar:** your avatar holds the tools, which fold out when you need them, and the bar folds away as you scroll down.
- **The header:** a numbered programme with a trailer frame on a PC, swipeable stacked cards on a phone, and a deck of panels on a TV.
- **Continue watching:** a list beside a large stage, with the episode fading in as a muted preview.
- **Next up:** a shelf of spines that swing open into covers.
- **Your own library rows:** choose a Shelf or Expanding look in Settings > Home.
- **Seerr rows and Recently added:** poster tiles that lift and tilt, with the request status as a pill.
- **This week:** the days on a timeline, with cards that fold out of the line. Days with several shows slide out the others on hover, or when held on a phone.
- **Browse by genre:** tiles that flip over, with a new selection each day.
- **Your libraries:** a door for each one, showing its artwork, that swings open.
- **Tabs:** Home, Movies and Shows, in the top bar on a PC or TV and along the bottom on a phone.
- **Sections on a drum:** each one rolls into view as you scroll.
- **The background:** blurred artwork that follows what you're looking at.
- **Effects, for slower devices:** Settings > Appearance > Effects has Full, Balanced and Lite.

### New in every theme
- **This week on Home:** what airs and comes out, Monday to Sunday. It reads Sonarr and Radarr through the Jellyfin Enhanced plugin if your server has it, with no keys in the app, and uses Jellyfin's own list otherwise. Choose the source in Settings > Home. It keeps itself current, and says when something's missing.
- **Browse by genre** on Home.
- **Fonts:** the theme's own, your device's, any Google Fonts family, or your own font file. Google Fonts is off unless you turn it on, so nothing contacts Google for fonts by default.
- **A new app icon** on Android, Android TV and Windows.

### Changed
- **The mouse wheel glides** on Home and library pages.
- **Signing out is instant,** even if the server is out of reach.
- **Home's top bar on phones:** buttons no longer run off the screen.
- **Windows draws with Skia,** for smoother movement on large windows.
- **Phones: Trailers and previews on mobile data** in Settings > Home covers both. Off means Wi-Fi only.
- **Download files** carry the version in their names.
- **Featured shows** are a better mix, with a new selection each time the app starts or you refresh.

### Fixed
- Home's header comes back by itself if the server was starting or unreachable.
- Refresh no longer leaves an old trailer playing in the header.
- Release notes in the update dialog no longer show garbled characters.
- A long row title ends in "…" instead of running off a phone's screen.

### Known issues
- **Depth on Android TV hasn't had its full test yet,** so some of it may not work well with a remote. Classic is unaffected.
- **Clicks stopped responding once on a PC** for a few minutes during testing, then recovered. We couldn't reproduce it. If you see it, please send the app log.
