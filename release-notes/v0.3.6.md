## Kurage v0.3.6: a new name, and everything since v0.3.5

This app was called **Jellyfin Client**. It's now **Kurage** (Japanese for jellyfish), an independent Jellyfin client, open source under the GNU GPL v3, with a jellyfish icon of its own.

### Moving over from Jellyfin Client
Kurage is a **new app**, not an update: install it alongside, then uninstall Jellyfin Client when you're happy.
- **Sign in again** once, on each device.
- **What's kept on your server comes back:** watched state and progress, your watchlist and its order, playlists, your MyAnimeList link and the audio and subtitle choices you made per show.
- **What starts fresh:** settings kept on the device (theme, look, Home rows, quality) and downloads. Theme files (Settings > Appearance > Export) can carry your look across.

> **Android TV:** the Android app installs and runs on Android TV, but its TV layout **hasn't been tested yet**.

### New since v0.3.5
- **Depth's pages for shows, films and episodes**, with the top bar and tabs, swipe actions on episodes, and a main button that knows whether to start, continue or resume.
- **Watchlist:** shows, episodes and films, in your own order (kept on your account), with filters, a grid or ranked list, and picking several at once.
- **Playlists:** add to one (or a new one) from anywhere, then arrange it on its own page: drag to reorder, pick several to move, mark or take out (with Undo). Playing from a playlist carries on through it.
- **Quality in the player:** Auto, Original or a limit; Auto steps down when playback keeps stopping to load. Settings > Playback sets the default. Subtitles stay while the server converts the video, and picture subtitles (PGS) can be burned in.
- **Up next** in the last 30 seconds, counting down to the end; **scenes after the credits** play instead of being skipped.
- **Theme files:** export your theme and import one.
- **When the server can't be reached,** the app says so plainly, keeps trying, and still offers your downloads.

### Fixed
- Skip and Up next show in fullscreen on Windows, and the pointer hides with the controls.
- A server restart mid-episode no longer sends playback back to the start.
- Windows no longer freezes after a theme switch or when opening a file picker.
- Progress watched offline is kept as you watch, and sent once the server is back.
- On a PC, holding a card does nothing; right-click is its menu.
