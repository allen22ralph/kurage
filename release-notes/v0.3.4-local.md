### Android phones: the navigation bar
- **Scrolling down** a page hides Android's buttons (◁ ○ ☰), as in Chrome.
- **Scrolling up**, reaching the top, or opening another page brings them back. A swipe up from the bottom edge always shows them.
- **Pages that can't scroll** (sign-in, dialogs) keep the bar, in the page's own colour, like Play Store.
- Rows that scroll sideways, the player, TVs and Windows are unchanged.

### One app in Recents
After an update (or opening the app from anywhere other than its icon), Android sometimes showed **two** Jellyfin Client cards in Recents. Now it's always one.

### Release notes, formatted
The update dialog shows these notes the way GitHub does: headings, **bold**, and lists. Links open in your browser.
